What do the skill labels mean?
Clear-cut modelThe business goal is stated and the math model is mostly fixed.
Design your own approachNo method is given: the agent must design its own objective, decomposition, heuristic, policy or rolling scheme.
Estimate from dataDemand, risk or scenario parameters have to be estimated from data.
Several periods or stepsDecisions span several periods or steps, such as batching, sequencing or rolling execution.
Uncertain inputsThe plan has to cope with several scenarios or uncertain inputs.
Tie-breaking rulesPlans that tie, or nearly tie, on the main goal must be ranked by a secondary rule.

A task can carry several labels. They describe the task and play no part in scoring.
