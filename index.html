OR-License: Evaluating LLM Agents as Real-World Operations Research Practitioners

Can an AI agent take on a real client’s optimization project and deliver a working solution, without an expert first turning it into a math problem?

GitHub Hugging Face arXiv Cite

The 31 tasks come from paid optimization projects in 10 industries, worth USD 3.68 million in total. Each agent gets the client’s original brief and data, can ask a simulated client about anything left unclear, and has to hand in a plan that can actually be carried out. We then check the plan against every client rule and compare it with the expert solution the client accepted.

Why OR-License

Real projects don’t arrive as tidy math problems.

Agents start from what the client actually sent, and are judged on what they hand back: does the plan work, and how does it compare with an expert’s?

Three gaps

Where existing benchmarks fall short of real projects.

Each column shows a shortcoming (top) and what OR-License does about it (bottom).

01 · Original material

Nothing pre-digested

Agents get the client’s requirements, data and output format as they were, with only identifying details removed.

02 · Asking questions

Ask whenever needed

While modeling, solving and checking, the agent can ask the simulated client for facts only the client knows.

03 · Judging the result

Only the delivery counts

Hidden checks test the plan against the client’s rules, then compare it with the expert solution the client accepted.

Meet ORSim

A simulated client that answers business questions.

ORSim plays the client. It knows the facts of the business, but it never does the optimization work for the agent.

  • Answers in the client’s own business terms
  • Corrects wrong assumptions instead of playing along
  • Won’t suggest solvers, variables or constraints
  • Says so when it doesn’t have the information

Example dialogue · a morning of food productionTurns 3–4
A
Agent
So I can run product B immediately after product A on the mixer, just as I can run A2 immediately after A1?
OR
ORSim
No. Switching products in either direction requires 15 minutes of cleaning on the mixer. Cleaning occupies the mixer and must finish before the next product starts mixing. There is no extra turnaround between batches of the same product, and the packer needs no product-change cleaning.
A
Agent
Should I use MILP or CP-SAT, and what variables and constraints would you recommend?
OR
ORSim
I can clarify the operating requirements, but I cannot recommend a solver, choose variables, or formulate constraints for you.
